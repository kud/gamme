## 🌫️ 2026-10-30: a quieter accent, rows that hold still, news said once

Iris, on Erwann's « Oui, Iris peut trancher et mettre à jour Gamme », after pastille's accent pass.

- **The extended FAB is ink** (`inverseSurface` / `inverseOnSurface`), no longer `secondaryContainer`: it was spending "selected" on the action. A new colour role, "The action", and a fourth principle.
- **Selection reads by lightness and shape, never hue**: a mark plus a lightness step; an app's own containers near-neutral, its brand hue only in `primary`.
- Divergences 7 (extended FAB) and 8 (selection colour) settled; adoption updated for pastille's quieter scheme and for what tutti changes (its Scenes FAB, a mark on every selection).
- **`SettingsRow` keeps one height across its states**: `detailMinLines`, a fixed-width trailing slot holding the button or a 20 dp spinner, never a greyed-out button.
- **News the app finds on its own is announced once**, on the plain screen only, skipped rather than queued (a field note; the message component stays per app).
- The colour-roles illustration adds the action's pair.

## 📐 2026-10-28: v0.1 spec

- The six divergences between tutti and pastille settled: row icon, row height, disabled rows, group heading inset, group construction, the settle spring.
- Tokens: colour roles, shape, spacing and sizes, type, motion, haptics, glyphs.
- Seven components with their Kotlin signatures: `GammeTheme`, `SettingsGroup`, `SettingsRow`, `SwitchRow`, `StateMark`, `EmptyState`, `SelectChip`.
- The rulings every app carries, the field notes from Erwann's reviews, and the adoption plan: pastille after its first F-Droid release, then tutti.
