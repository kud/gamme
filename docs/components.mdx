---

Only what both apps use today. Signatures as they should look in the kit; bodies are the implementer's, within the rules under each.

- 🌗 [GammeTheme](/projects/gamme/docs/theme): The theme root: scheme, dynamic colour, true black, shapes, type and live reduced motion.
- 🗂️ [SettingsGroup](/projects/gamme/docs/settings-group): A heading and a clipped card of rows, 2 dp apart, aligned to one glyph slot.
- 📋 [SettingsRow](/projects/gamme/docs/settings-row): One row, one target: read-only, opens, unavailable with its reason, notice, destructive, or a radio.
- 🔘 [SwitchRow](/projects/gamme/docs/switch-row): A row that toggles: the row is the only target, the switch only draws state.
- ✅ [StateMark](/projects/gamme/docs/state-mark): Six 16 dp state marks; the words beside them carry the state.
- 📭 [EmptyState](/projects/gamme/docs/empty-state): A disc, a title, a detail and at most one action.
- 🏷️ [SelectChip](/projects/gamme/docs/select-chip): A filter or choice chip whose width never changes when it's selected.

## ⏳ Not in v0.1

Sheet, passing message, menu, top bar, segmented control wrapper, toggle and action chips, list card, extended FAB rules, banner, press/focus indication, `RevealRow`, the copy tests. Each app keeps its own until the second app needs the same thing; the draft holds their direction.
